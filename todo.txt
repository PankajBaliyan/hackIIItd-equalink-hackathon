  score_after, with a "+N points from validated fixes" line. No second ring when no fix stage ran (no OPENAI_API_KEY).
  Each ring has its own SVG gradient id. (client/src/components/scan/scoreView.ts, ScoreRing.tsx, LiveScan.tsx)
